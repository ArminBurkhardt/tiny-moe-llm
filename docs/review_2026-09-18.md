# Codebase review before the Phase 4 spend (2026-09-18)

A read of the plan ([NEXT.md](plans/NEXT.md)), every measurement record under
[measurements/](measurements/), the model flow, the evidence port, the corpus builder, the
trainer and the evidence smoke log, cross-checked against RETRO, Fusion-in-Decoder, Memory Layers
at Scale and the 2026 looped-LM papers listed at the end.

**Headline.** The Phase 4 run as built will very likely miss G3 and G3b for structural reasons,
not training reasons. Three of those reasons are fixable in a day each and should land before the
11-hour corpus build, because two of them change the on-disk format. Below that, the repo's
conclusion that a parametric memory "cannot" learn facts from LM cross-entropy is contradicted by
the literature and should not drive the real run's spec.

---

## 1. Before the Phase 4 spend (ordered by severity)

### 1.1 The reader attends over uncontextualized 32-d token embeddings

`TinyMoETransformer.build_evidence` ([transformer.py](../modules/model/transformer.py)) builds
the evidence states with `_moe_ple`, i.e. the 65536 x 32 `moe_embeddings` table projected to 768
by one linear. No decoder layer ever touches the evidence, and the states do not change across
loops.

The 3.23-nat ceiling in [evidence_ceiling.md](measurements/evidence_ceiling.md) was measured with
the passage going through all 8 decoder layers plus 3 loops of the MoE block. A reader over a bag
of token identities with per-chunk RoPE can copy a token it already expects, but it cannot find
"the token after *born in*": no key carries any context, and the per-chunk position basis means
positions are not comparable to the query side either.

Every published side-channel design encodes the neighbours with a real encoder. RETRO runs a
bidirectional encoder over the retrieved neighbours before chunked cross-attention. Fusion-in-
Decoder encodes each passage independently (linear in passage count) and cross-attends in the
decoder. Nobody attends to raw token embeddings, for this reason.

**Fix.** Run the evidence chunks through the dense decoder once per forward (no loops, no LM head),
non-causal or causal per chunk, and cache the result across the three loops. Cost is roughly
+2.9 x 122 MFLOP per prompt token at the corpus's measured evidence ratio, about +70% forward,
still linear in evidence with no quadratic term. A cheaper variant reuses only the first 2 to 4
decoder layers.

**De-risk first.** A 5M-token smoke A/B, PLE-embedded evidence against decoder-encoded evidence,
reading the gold-vs-none CE gap on the val split. If the PLE reader closes less than a third of
the ceiling, the design is the problem and no amount of the 150M-token run fixes it.

### 1.2 The seed is a pretrained trunk, not a chat model

The smoke seeds from `checkpoint_ir_final_evidence.pt`, which descends from `phase2_final` via
the IR sharpening arm C. It has never seen the chat template: step 0 of the smoke log reads CE
6.57 on chat-formatted data. G3 and G3b are read through `eval_abstention.py`, i.e. through the
chat template, against numbers measured on the repair checkpoint (probe AUROC 0.584, precision
0.578). The run as planned would be learning the chat template, the abstention policy and the port
at once at lr 1e-5, and every delta would be confounded with the first two.

**Fix.** The table stores nothing (Stage 0), so the reshape is free. Migrate
`checkpoint_repair_final.pt` with `migrate_ir_reshape.py --arm random`, then
`migrate_evidence_port.py`, and seed Phase 4 from that.

### 1.3 Bug: the IR table is in the fresh-LR group for the evidence profile

`build_sft_param_groups` in [sft.py](../scripts/sft.py) computes
`is_fresh = is_rebuilt_ir_param(name) or is_fresh_loop_param(name)` whenever `fresh_lr` is set.
`is_rebuilt_ir_param` matches the whole `ir_module` subtree, so `z_keys`, `y_values`, `g_proj`,
`down_proj`, `up_proj` and `log_temperature` train at 3e-4 in the evidence profile. The smoke log's
"11 fresh/decayed, 3 fresh/undecayed" is exactly the port (6 + 2) plus the table (5 + 1).
`EvidenceConfig`'s docstring in [config.py](../config.py) and `migrate_evidence_port.py`'s report
both claim the opposite.

**Fix.** A per-profile predicate: the evidence profile's fresh set is `is_fresh_loop_param` only.

### 1.4 Nothing trains the selector toward relevance

The IR expert is routed, and the aux loss pins its selection at 1/35 by construction. Its value
path then passes `g_proj` (stalled at RMS 0.0047, [ir_scale_fix.md](measurements/ir_scale_fix.md)),
`up_proj`, the expert's inner cross-attention, and the router gate. So `key_adapter`,
`value_adapter` and `log_memory_scale` receive gradient through ~6% of tokens times a ~200x
attenuated valve, and the mass split that G3b reads is trained by nothing direct.

The corpus knows which chunk is gold; `apply_condition` shuffles it into the distractors and then
forgets which one it was.

**Fix.** Store a per-chunk gold flag (one more `.evgold` uint8 file next to `.evkey`, indexed by the
same chunk axis) and add a selection loss on the per-chunk external mass at the supervised
positions (InfoNCE or BCE over the chunks visible to that document). It is loadable-neutral, cheap,
and trains the selector regardless of routing. This is a deviation from "one objective through
`pretrain.train_step` verbatim" and is worth it.

### 1.5 G3b cannot discriminate SQuAD v2 with the signal as designed

SQuAD v2's unanswerable questions were written adversarially against a *relevant* passage, so the
cosine between a query and bge(passage) is high in both classes. The external-mass split will
separate "evidence attached vs none", not "answerable vs not". The only component that can see
whether the answer span is present is the reader.

**Fix.** Train a small groundedness head on the reader's output at the last prompt position with
the non-leaky label "gold chunk present and the row is answerable", which the corpus also knows.
This is the correctness-head pattern with an *external* label instead of the model's own argmax,
so it cannot collapse onto `p_max` the way `correct_proj` did. Keep G3b's 0.65 bar; read it on this
head and on the mass split separately.

### 1.6 Conversation weighting inverts the mix

By conversation count the corpus is roughly QA 80%, web text 9%, chat replay 11% (QA ~100 tokens
per conversation, web text >= 384 and typically ~1,000, chat ~500). Under `1 / n_supervised` the
20% replay floor is ~11% of the gradient, and the web-text arm, the only source that generalizes
the port past QA formatting, is ~9%. Retune against the realized counts the builder prints.

**Fix.** Apply conversation weighting to the QA sources only, or cap the per-conversation weight
(e.g. `1 / sqrt(n)` or a floor of 1/64 per token).

### 1.7 The loop-conditioned IR query is not built

Stage 0 promoted it from a refinement to a precondition (`cos(q2, q3) = 0.99`). Phase 4 re-reads
the evidence at every loop with a query that does not move after loop 1, so the run tests reading,
not recursion over evidence, which is the stated objective. It is a zero-init sinusoidal bias
mirroring `loop_router_bias`, a day of work, and neutral to load. Also note `last_memory_mass` is
overwritten every loop, so the G3b readout is the final loop's only.

### 1.8 Per-condition validation is missing, and it is the early-kill number

The val pass reports one CE. Gold-vs-none CE gap on the val split *is* G3, and reading it at 10M
tokens is the kill criterion. Store the condition label per document (a `.cond` uint8 file) now,
before the 11-hour build, and report val CE per condition. Also log `|shared_evidence.o_proj|rms`
against its zero init (the same reason `|g_proj|rms` is logged) and mean external mass per
condition.

### 1.9 Buffer sizes never exceed ~10 chunks

SQuAD `mixed` is gold plus 3 distractors; HotpotQA is 10 paragraphs; the builder drops rows whose
evidence exceeds 1,536 tokens, which will drop many HotpotQA `mixed` rows and skew the hardest
condition out of the corpus. G6 wants 64+ chunks and the reader never sees more than ten.

**Fix.** Raise the builder cap to ~2,048, check the `too_long` counts after the build, and add a
many-distractor condition (16 to 32 chunks) on a subset. Evidence tokens stay cheap enough for
this even with 1.1's encoder.

### 1.10 Smaller items

- Reader K/V are recomputed at every loop for evidence states that never change across loops;
  compute them once per forward.
- `SQUAD_INSTRUCTION` says "the passage below" while nothing is below in evidence mode. Either
  extend the instruction consistently in the builder and the eval, or accept the mismatch.
- The `{phase} tokens` log field counts prompt tokens, not evidence tokens.
- `.evidence_ceiling_probe.py` is referenced by a tracked doc but is untracked; move it under
  `scripts/`.
- The smoke log's per-loop CE has loop 3 worse than loop 2 at several log steps (step 0: 6.61 /
  6.41 / 6.57). Out of distribution the last loop hurts; worth reading per condition once 1.8
  exists.

## 2. The real run and the architecture

### 2.1 "A parametric table cannot learn facts from LM CE" is contradicted by Memory Layers at Scale

Berges et al. (ICLR 2025) train a shared product-key memory replacing 3 FFN layers from scratch at
134M to 1.3B parameters and get 2 to 4x NQ and TriviaQA accuracy at matched compute, with a 1.3B
memory model approaching Llama2-7B on factual QA. The differences to this repo's IR expert are the
whole story:

| | Memory+ (Berges et al.) | this repo's IR expert |
|---|---|---|
| value dim | hidden size | 384, then `g_proj`, then `up_proj` |
| output path | silu-gated direct residual add | inner cross-attention over per-token reads, then the router gate |
| selection | exact top-k over 1M to 64M product keys | 256 centroids, 8 probed, refresh, recall warnings |
| routing | always-on | top-2 of 35, aux loss pins it at 1/35 |
| training | from scratch, 1T tokens | grafted onto a converged trunk, 208M tokens |

The read here passes four attenuators in series. Three grafted arms agreeing to four decimals is
evidence about the graft and the pathway, not about whether LM CE can teach a memory. For the
stated objective (facts in the IR expert, language in the trunk) the real run should build the IR
expert as a Memory+ style layer: from scratch, in the residual path, always-on, value dim equal to
hidden, product-key exact top-k, reused at every loop with the loop-conditioned query. Do not freeze
it out of the spec on the strength of Phase 3.

### 2.2 Too much attention per loop, and the doubtful modules are the expensive ones

Each loop runs four attention modules (shared attention, self-attention expert, cross-attention
over the PLE embeddings, the IR expert's inner attention), five with the reader. Attention is 126
of 499 MFLOP forward. The cross-attention over token-identity embeddings and the IR inner
attention (attention over what *other* tokens retrieved) have no published analogue and no measured
value. Ablate both in the from-scratch A/B, and let the IR expert write to the residual directly.

### 2.3 The shape is inverted relative to every published looped design

Eight dense prelude layers feed one looped block: the recurrent core is ~150 of ~500 MFLOP per
token. Geiping, Mixture-of-Recursions and SMELT all put most of the compute in the recurrent stack
with a short prelude and a coda. The 7c A/B should compare "4 prelude + a 2-block looped core x 3 +
2 coda" at matched compute, not only "coda vs none".

### 2.4 The readout blind spot

"Dense Supervision Is Not Enough" (arXiv 2606.24898) shows that RMSNorm readouts hide the state
norm from per-loop CE while the recurrence amplifies it, and that supervision alone cannot control
recurrent scale. Here `‖Δh‖/‖h‖` falls 0.87 / 0.36 / 0.10 across loops and `loop_scale` collapsed
under the halt gate. Log `‖h‖` per loop, and for any depth curriculum past 3 either normalize the
state at loop entry or make the scale visible to the loss.

### 2.5 Throughput

MFU 11% is mostly `hidden_size` 768: skinny GEMMs. FP8 on 768-wide layers will buy little, so 7a's
order should be: profile the step first, `torch.compile` the dense decoder and shared MLP, a bigger
micro-batch on the 80GB card, then the FP8 A/B. The heads are 100 of 499 MFLOP forward at 4x cost
(fwd, recompute, bwd), so tying the head plus a fused linear-CE plus subsampling the MTP heads (7d)
is the largest single win and also removes 13M parameters of rank-192 head. Run the oracle-head
reading before the spec is written.

### 2.6 Router specialization

The aux loss sat at its balanced value from step 0 with 32 experts at top-2, so each MLP expert saw
~1B tokens and learned roughly the same function. Measure `aux_loss_weight` sensitivity on the
existing checkpoint, and for the real run prefer fewer, larger experts or a bias-based balancing
that does not push the three non-MLP slots toward 1/35.

## 3. Suggested order, and three questions

**Before the corpus build** (format changes): 1.3, 1.6, 1.8, 1.9, and the gold flag from 1.4.

**Before the 2-hour run:** 1.1 (the 5M-token A/B decides it), 1.2, 1.4, 1.5, 1.7.

**During the run:** kill at 10M tokens if the gold-vs-none val gap is under ~0.1 nats.

Questions whose answer changes the work:

1. Seed Phase 4 from the repair checkpoint (recommended) or keep the arm C lineage for continuity
   with the IR arms?
2. Is encoding evidence through the dense decoder acceptable? It breaks "evidence tokens are cheap"
   but keeps the cost linear, and the port is unlikely to reach the ceiling without it.
3. Are a supervised selection loss and a groundedness head acceptable deviations from "one
   objective through `train_step`"? Both use labels the corpus already knows, not the model's own
   outputs.
