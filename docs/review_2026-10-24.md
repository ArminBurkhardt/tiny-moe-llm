# Full repository review, 2026-10-24

A review of the whole tree (model, forward pass, trainers, datasets, builders, evaluation scripts,
plan files, design page and the session memory) against the objective stated in
[plans/NEXT.md](plans/NEXT.md): facts come from the external store through the retrieval pathway,
the trunk holds language and reasoning, and reasoning over the retrieved facts happens in the
looped block. Six detached reviewers (one per area) read the code; the verdicts, the ordering and
the decisions below are the main session's. The pending work was committed first (`fee6a5c`, the
bridge builder and eval mode with the arm E4 record; `af24c70`, the eval packer fix for left
padding). Rules for what may land: the live run is untouched; a change to `modules/model`,
`modules/data`, the two trainers or an eval script lands only if its default behaviour is byte
identical, otherwise it waits for the bridge arm and its reads; docs, tooling, tests and builders
not in flight are free.

The written-bridge arm (R9) was running during the review, at 55M of 72M tokens, with reads on the
10M to 40M saves on disk (taken by `ckpts/inject/read_bridge_save.sh`). Its 30M read is quoted
below because it changes the plan; its record is the other session's to write.

## Verdict on the objective

**Externalization is demonstrated at pilot shape, relative to a matched control.** Arm P stores
the injected biographies at 1/47 of the control in distribution and 1/4 held out at tier 100, with
the tier 1000 residual recorded as known. That is the same kind of residual the published relatives
(LMLM, Co-LMLM) report against a standard model, and it is read with a sharper instrument. The
reachable form of the goal is "most facts externalized, a known residual", not "none in the
weights", and the plan already says so. Two of this review's data findings are concrete candidates
for what writes the residual (below, "Leak candidates"), each testable on the micro recipe in about
1.5 hours.

**Reasoning composes in written tokens, not in the latent loop, at this shape.** Every latent
composition cell read at chance across 100M chain tokens plus one more epoch; the bridge arm at
30M tokens reads held-out 2-hop 0.989 (chance 0.191), 3-hop 0.990 and the 1-hop gate 0.998 with
one read site kept, and the loop adds +0.004. The loop's measured value is language (+0.09 nats
at pass 2) and recall of weakly stored facts, not multi-hop composition. The goal's reasoning
clause should read "reasoning in written tokens, the loop as computation per token", which is
what 6f already plans. Under the user's own staging rule the R4 coda corner (a half-day build plus
up to five 1B-token arms) is now optional; skipping it is the single largest saving left in the
pilot ladder.

**The retriever and the abstention behaviour are the open halves, and both verdicts rest on reads
this review found flawed.** The A3 generation verdict ("96% abstention under gold") and the
pathway and edit reads were taken with the left-padded evidence buffer misread (finding 1). The
selector's training signal is dominated by biography tokens and the contrastive term spends two
thirds of its weight on a shortcut (findings 7 and 8), so the "no retriever term" decision was
made on an arm that never trained the term where it could work. Both get a cheap re-read before
the real-run spec is written.

**The path is achievable and mostly efficient.** What is not efficient: 18% of each pilot arm's
wall clock is the eval pass; the evidence encoder runs over thousands of padding tokens per row;
dense-expert picks still go through an MLP expert at weight zero; and two pilot-corpus flaws
(findings 2 and 3) make the held-out CE and A6 comparisons between arm P and arm C weaker than
recorded. The rank instruments, which decide A1, are unaffected.

## Findings that change a recorded reading

Ordered by what they touch. "Status" says what happened to each today.

1. **Every generation read with evidence attached before `af24c70` read a shifted buffer.**
   `eval_abstention._pack_evidence_batch` put a left-padded row's own evidence before the dump
   width, and `evidence_cu_seqlens` counts tokens per segment in sorted order, so the real segment
   read its own tail plus padding, and only padding when its buffer was shorter than half the
   batch maximum. Affected: `ladder_pilot.sh`'s A3 generation read (96% abstention under gold,
   EM 0.01, the follow rate, `mr_gen`), the G3b `last` mass at loops past 0, the A7 pathway EM
   (oracle 2.6%) and the edit flip rate, and the pre-fix bridge smoke (moved to
   `ckpts/inject/bridge_prefix_bug/`). Not affected (right padded): the teacher-forced G3 CE,
   `mr_ll`, the A3 likelihood read (2.13 against 5.87), every `closed_book_rank` read, plain
   `eval_chains`, `eval_store recall`. SQuAD passage chunk counts vary widely, so the abstention
   on short-buffer rows is plausibly the artefact, and the "A3 unreadable in generation" verdict
   and the 6f decision that rests on it ("the one RL step that must not fail") are both to be
   re-read. Status: code fixed this morning by the other session; the re-read waits for the GPU.
2. **Arm C trained on 50.1M tokens arms P, E4 and PI never saw, and finished its cosine where
   they stopped at 93%.** The evidence QA builder caps a windowed web row at 4094 tokens; the
   evidence reader packs to 4095 and adds the two MTP pads, so 4094 + 2 > 4095 drops every such
   row (12,240 documents, 50.11M tokens, 23.8% of the QA slice, in all three pilot splits, the
   same count in each), while the plain reader the control uses keeps them (4096 is allowed).
   `estimate_packed_rows` still counts them, so the evidence arms' `total_steps` is 7% high and
   their LR ends at 6.55e-5 instead of the 6.0e-5 floor. Arm P's 860.6M against arm C's 909.7M
   is exactly this. P against E4 stays matched (both drop them). P against C is matched on the
   biographies and the chains, not on the QA slice or the schedule, so the control's 0.013 CE
   edge on `inject_val` and the A6 within-noise read carry this caveat; the paired rank reads
   (z -81) do not. Status: recorded; no change to the reader today, because the next pilot-shape
   arm must choose between matching arm P (keep dropping) and keeping the tokens, and that is a
   spec decision. The row estimator fix (replay the reader's rule) changes the schedule and waits
   with it. The merge tool now reports documents over the evidence reader's limit.
3. **`inject_val` is partly a training read.** Its windows are drawn from the same `ir.bin`
   documents the evidence QA builder cuts its web rows from, and the exclusion is per window, not
   per document. The reviewer measured about 7.4% of `inject_val` tokens appearing verbatim as
   supervised continuation text in rows both arms train on (two passes), and another 6.7% in the
   dropped 4094-token rows only arm C trains on. Every held-out CE on `inject_val` (the pilot's
   language bar, arm PI's stop, the E4 read) is biased toward whichever arm saw more of it. Status:
   recorded; the fix is a validation slice cut from documents neither builder used, then a re-read
   of the three finals with `eval_exit.py`; proposed, not built (a corpus decision).
4. **The logged training CE, the per-loop CE and the `[eval]` CE on `inject_val` are accumulated
   in bf16.** The unweighted path of `_chunked_linear_ce` sums a bf16 cross entropy with
   `reduction="sum"` into a bf16 zero and divides by an integer cast to bf16; the per-batch CE sits
   on a 1/64-nat grid in [2, 4) and `top1_acc` can read 1.0078 (the first bf16 value above 1).
   The 40-batch eval mean carries about 0.002 of this against the pilot's 0.013 noise floor. The
   weighted `[eval fixed]` path and `eval_exit.py` are fp32 and unaffected; the gradient path is
   unaffected except at rounding. Status: fix written and held until the bridge arm is done
   (it changes logged numbers).
5. **The closed-book sigma is understated by clustering and `compare` pairs the wrong quantity.**
   The bootstrap resamples items, but one person contributes three entity items (five in
   `compare`) that share exposure and stored-or-not, so the item sigma is low by up to 1.7x (2.2x
   in `compare`). The A1(b) arm P cells (tier 100 held out z 3.2, in distribution z 2.2) can land
   under 3 when clustered; the decided reading (passes with the leak known, under a quarter of the
   control) does not move, because the control's cells are at z 40 to 80. `compare` differences
   the raw rank, not the prior-controlled `delta`, so a prior shift between arms reads as storage
   (it did in `compare_cf_vs_cf30`: raw z -4.0 against `delta` z -2.1); it also pools entity, date
   and noun where A1 reads entities only, and its `all` row counts each fact twice over the two
   forms. Status: person-clustered sigma and a paired `delta` compare added as extra output lines,
   the existing lines unchanged, and run on the pilot finals' JSONs: clustering moves the sigma by
   0.9x to 1.1x only, so arm P's cells stand (held out tier 100 z 3.2 item and clustered; tier
   1000 z 7.2 item, 8.0 clustered in distribution); on entities only the control against arm P at
   held-out tier 100 reads raw z -3.8 (clustered -4.0) and `delta` z +7.0 (clustered +6.5), both
   saying the control stores more. The 1.7x bound was the worst case, not the data.
6. **A 2-hop shortcut scores 0.256 where chance is 0.185.** In the chain buffers the gold, decoy
   and B-distractor final subjects are fed by the second-to-last hop while the A-distractor
   feeders are random, so picking the final object whose subject is itself an object of a
   second-to-last-hop chunk scores 0.256 on seen 2-hop, 0.257 held out and 0.294 on 3-hop without
   reading the start entity (measured on regenerated eval questions, byte equal to the built
   file). Today's at-chance verdicts are unchanged; any future above-chance latent read must beat
   this baseline, not 1 over K. The held-out composition set at seed 42 is also only three pairs,
   all with `founder` as the first relation. Status: the shortcut picker is added to the chain
   builder's printed baselines; the held-out draw stays as built (a rebuild changes the splits
   the pilot arms were read on).
7. **The selection loss is averaged per predicting token, so long biography documents carry it.**
   On 30k sampled documents of `pilot_main_train`, documents of 600 tokens or more are 1.8% of
   the evidence documents and carry 63% of the gold-document supervised positions; the chain and
   QA rows (79% of evidence documents) carry 16.5%. The selector's relevance signal is mostly
   biography text, which is the shape of the A4 result (recall@5 0.004). Also: `p.clamp(eps, 1 -
   eps)` zeroes the gradient once the gold chunk's share is under 1e-6, so a confidently wrong
   ranking gets no gradient, and single-chunk rows contribute a constant that still counts in the
   denominator; the logged `selection:` swings 0.25 to 0.70 with batch composition late in arm P.
   Status: recorded; the per-segment weighting and the log-space loss change numbers and wait.
8. **The contrastive term at loops 2 and 3 is solvable from evidence the position already read.**
   Under the key/value reader `shared_attn` reads the prefix keys in loop 1, so at later loops the
   IR query position carries the chunk content, and in-batch InfoNCE there can be matched on
   content instead of the question; store recall is read at loop 1. The default
   `evidence_contrastive_loops: null` spends two thirds of the term on that shortcut, whose
   gradient goes through the trunk, which fits arm PI's 0.30-nat language cost (an inference, not
   a measurement). The untested cell is loops `[0]` with the trunk gradient on. Status: recorded;
   the default stays (weight 0 makes it inert), the micro arm is proposed below.
9. **Under the key/value reader the groundedness head reads the whole attention output.**
   `last_reader_output` is `shared_attn`'s output (own context plus evidence), nonzero from step
   0, so "its weight gradient is zero until `o_proj` leaves zero" is false for that reader, the BCE
   gradient reaches the trunk's own attention, and the head can read the label off the prompt
   context or the base rate. Status: the CLAUDE.md statement is corrected; an evidence-only
   signal (the prefix-only softmax mass from a second flash call's log-sum-exp) is the fix and
   waits.

## Leak candidates (what writes the tier 1000 level)

Two data-side findings supervise the real name with the real value outside the card path:

- **Value-first templates train value-to-name recall with weight 1 in every arm.** Templates such
  as "{v} is the hometown of {s}." and "{v} employs {s}." put the name after the value; the name
  mask is always 1, so when such a sentence opens a biography the name is predicted from the value
  before the name is in context. The closed-book instrument probes name-to-value only, so it
  cannot see this direction. A cheap read: a value-to-name probe (prompt with the value, rank the
  names) on arm P's final; a cheap fix: mask the name on its first mention, or keep value-first
  templates out of position 0.
- **Gold-absent documents count a value as supported when it collides with a distractor card.**
  About 5.6% of gold-absent spans, 0.56% of all spans, supervise the real name with the real
  value with no gold card present. Small, but it is exactly the kind of exposure the warm-up arm's
  150M to 300M growth at tier 1000 needs (about 0.68 of exposures were real-name real-value under
  the old count; the placeholder arm halved that and moved nothing, so this is a long shot).

Both are builder changes (bytes change) on a builder not in flight; neither is built today, both
are one micro warm-up arm each (about 1.5 hours) if the user wants the tier 1000 question reopened.

## Bugs and inert paths that do not touch a recorded number

  `moe.balance_bias`), because the tolerated key lands in its "added" set. Fixed today.
- `evidence_cu_seqlens` assumed the evidence axis is sorted by segment and never checked; an
  unsorted packer (the bug fixed in `af24c70`) silently pointed documents at each other's

- `sft.py` does not persist `train_split`, `val_split` or `data_dir`; the pilot launchers default
  to the warm-up split, so a post-switch relaunch without `SPLIT=` silently resumes on swap-rate

- The fp32 masters are not checkpointed and are reseeded from bf16 on every relaunch; late in the

  to half an ulp of progress per weight (arm P relaunched eight times). Today: the masters go into
  the payload under `fp32_masters` (about 0.4 GB at 99M) and are restored when present; older

- The depth-sampling RNG restarts from the seed on every relaunch and the CUDA RNG (the loop-CE

- `_chunked_linear_ce` branches on `n_valid == 0` on a device tensor, five host syncs per step.

- `sft.py` writes `run_state.json` but never calls `verify_resume`; relaunching a finished run
  resumes from `_final.pt` and overwrites it. Today: a finished run exits 0 with a message.
- Under `--generate-bridge`, `eval_chains.py` prints the latent instrument's sub-hop rule ("a
  cell with fewer kept sites than hops must sit at chance") and declared the 30M read `instrument

## Efficiency, ranked by expected gain at pilot shape

Numbers are the reviewers' estimates; none is measured. Items marked "exact" change no real-token
output and could land now; they are held with the rest until the GPU is free for the bit-identity
tests, then land as one commit.

1. **The eval pass is about 18% of wall clock** (2.1 minutes every 25M tokens on arm E4, about 9
   minutes of training). The fixed pass reruns the three-loop head over all positions once per
   condition and again per condition and source, about 41 head passes per batch over rows 17% full.
   One `reduction="none"` CE vector per loop, gathered at the supervised positions and summed per
   partition, brings it under 30 seconds. Changes no number.
2. **Evidence padding is encoded as one long causal segment.** Every row's trailing `-1` run
   (thousands of tokens when a biography row shares a batch with a chain or QA row at the 4608
   cap) goes through all six decoder layers with quadratic attention, and nothing reads the
   result. Forcing a chunk boundary at every padding token removes the quadratic part exactly;
   unpadding the encoder input removes the rest (kernel-choice differences only).
3. **Dense-expert picks run through MLP expert 0 at weight 0.** Every (token, slot) that picked
   an attention or IR expert is remapped to `(0, weight 0)` and still goes through the grouped
   GEMM; at the measured dense share (0.35 to 0.54 of picks under the fixed aux term, less under
   loss-free) that is up to half of the routed MLP FLOPs, roughly 11% of the step. Sorting the
   null slots to the end and slicing them off before the GEMM is exact in value (cuBLAS may pick
   another kernel for the smaller M).
4. **The frozen chunk gate still costs three passes of work.** Every config sets
   `freeze_evidence_gate: true`, so the gate is exactly 1, yet each loop builds the `[T, M]`
   selector mass, the argsort and lookup, multiplies the states by ones, keeps an extra
   `[B, S_ev, H]` for backward and backpropagates zeros into the IR weights. Skipping it when the
   scale is zero and frozen is exact.
5. **The evidence norm, key and value projections and the prefix rotation are recomputed per
   loop** although only the per-token gate and the value scale change between loops; the
   interleave index math in `prefix_varlen_attention` and the two rank reconciliations in
   `evidence.py` are recomputed per loop too. Computing them once per forward saves two loops'
   worth of prefix work and activations (rounding only, once the gate scale is nonzero).
6. **The dense `[T, M]` selector tensors** (M about 650 keys per batch at pilot shape while each
   token sees at most 17) are built in fp32 per loop for the joint softmax, the selection BCE, the
   chunk mean mass, the visibility casts and the groundedness matmul, an estimated 1 to 2 GB kept
   for backward. A local `[T, K_max]` gather, or the selection loss on supervised rows only,
   removes most of it.
7. **The training LM head runs over unsupervised positions** (QA and chain prompts); gathering the
   supervised positions before the final-loop head saves an estimated 3 to 8%.
8. **Host syncs**: the five `n_valid` syncs (removed today), the pageable H2D copy
   (`pin_memory=True` plus non-blocking), the boolean mask in `evidence_from_batch`, the
   `positions.nonzero()` in the contrastive term, and an upper-bound `max_seqlen` that sizes every
   flash grid at the full row (one true-maximum sync per step would shrink about 15 attention
   calls). 1 to 4%.
9. **Optimizer bookkeeping**: `torch._foreach_copy_` for the master copies and `fused=True` AdamW,
   about 1%.
10. **Eval-side**: `closed_book_rank` and plain `eval_chains` re-encode the same buffer once per
    candidate (6 to 100x); encoding once per question and replicating the states would make the
    rank read several times faster. The eval path also stacks and norms every loop's hidden state
    to use the last.

Items 1 to 4 together are plausibly 30 to 40% of a pilot arm's wall clock. They are worth doing
before R6 (1 to 2B tokens) and before the real run's throughput work in 6a, which starts from the
same code.

## What the plan should change

- **R4's coda corner is optional now** (the user's rule of 2026-10-23: it runs only if R9 says
  the loop, not written tokens, must compose). The bridge composes at one read site. Recommended:
  do not run it; R4 waits for the real run's size, as the rule's last clause says.
- **R4b's sublayer half should not run at pilot scale.** Arm E4 showed 4 against 8 experts
  indistinguishable at a 0.013 noise floor with two changes and one seed; a 2-against-3 sublayer
  read at the same floor, after a day of architecture work and two 1B arms, would read the same
  way. Recommended: pick the sublayer count on cost and the reader-site argument (two sites per
  pass) and record it as a cost choice, as the expert count now is.
- **R6 stays**: it is the only read of pretraining-text facts (not injected biographies) reaching
  the weights, and the real run's leak claim is about those.
- **Before the next pilot-shape arm, fix the two corpus flaws** (the dropped 4094-token rows and
  the `inject_val` contamination) and decide how arms after P are matched to it.
- **Two cheap micro arms before the real-run spec closes "no retriever term"**: the contrastive
  term at loop 1 only with the trunk gradient on (the untested cell of finding 8), and the
  selection loss weighted per segment (finding 7), each read on store recall and held-out CE
  against `w0e3`. About 1.5 hours each on the micro recipe.
- **Re-read A3 in generation, the A7 pathway read and the A4 edit read** on arm P's final with
  the fixed packer before 6f's abstention reward is called the step that must not fail.
- **Gate names**: the gate table defines A4 as the store edit (flip rate) and A7 as model-query
  recall; every record since 2026-10-14 calls the recall read "A4" and the oracle EM read "A7".
  The records are renamed to match the table.
- **The two "Now" sections** are one 200-line chronological paragraph each with superseded
  "launched" and "nothing run" sentences beside the done state, NEXT.md's header still says
  2026-10-14, and "next" is split over three places. Restructured today: a dated state block at
  the top (running, just read, next action, open user decisions), the history under a "Record"
  heading, one shared table of open items mirrored in both files.
